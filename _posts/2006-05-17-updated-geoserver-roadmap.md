Hey, just wanted to let everyone know I just updated the [Roadmap](https://web.archive.org/web/20060624172623/http://docs.codehaus.org/display/GEOS/Roadmap) with the latest known plans of the GeoServer community. Sorry it had gotten so out of date, we will make an effort to keep it more in sync in the future. Note that this time no set numbers are given, we are just sorting projects in to those which are funded and will finish in less than 3 months, those that are longer term funded efforts, and those that are currently unfunded. Note that anyone can jump aboard and join the community to influence what comes on when. The more resources going to an effort, the sooner it will get out.
