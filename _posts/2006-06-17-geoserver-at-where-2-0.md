This week Brent of [TOPP](http://topp.openplans.org) gave a talk with Arnulf Christl of [MapBender](http://mapbender.osgeo.org) about open source mashups and GeoServer at the [Where 2.0](http://conferences.oreillynet.com/where2006/) conference in San Jose. You can see a picture of them on [flickr](http://flickr.com/photos/x180/167199394/). Chris Holmes was also out for an [OSGeo](http://osgeo.org) board meeting, and helped with bringing more publicity to OSGeo at the conference, as members showed up in black shirts and manned a big booth with information. There's a nice [article](http://www.wired.com/news/technology/0,71170-0.html?tw=wn_index_1) in Wired about the conference.
