Just a quick note that we updated the extra datastore plug-ins. These will work with GeoServer 1.3.1 and above (they are built off of GeoTools 2.2.x) - the current crop became incompatible with the latest release. The extras include ArcSDE (we're sticking in its own until we can build off of good dummy jars - though it should work well, including a fix for kml stuff), DB2, MySQL, WFS, and MySQL. They can be downloaded at [sourceforge](https://sourceforge.net/projects/geoserver/files/OldFiles/extensions/DataStore%20Extras%201.3.x%2B/)
