Chris Holmes gave a tech talk at Googl: [GeoServer and Architectures of Participation for Geospatial Information](http://video.google.com/videoplay?docid=-3923618086187138447) (click on the link to see the full talk on google video). It talks about GeoServer and how [TOPP](https://web.archive.org/web/20060830/http://topp.openplans.org) leveraged open standards to interoperate with Google Earth.

You can also see and (re)use the slides [powerpoint](https://web.archive.org/web/20060830/http://docs.codehaus.org/download/attachments/61286/geoserver-tech-talk-ch.ppt) and [open document](https://web.archive.org/web/20060830/http://docs.codehaus.org/download/attachments/61286/geoserver-tech-talk-ch.odp) - under a [creative commons attribution](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/) license.
